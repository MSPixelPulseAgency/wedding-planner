# Content Agent

## Voice

Write for LUMA Weddings & Events in simple, calm, emotionally intelligent English. The tone is warm, editorial and specific—premium without being distant.

## Content system

Service copy lives in `src/data/siteData.js`, alongside portfolio concepts, FAQs, testimonials and journal posts. Preserve the page structure and use descriptive internal links between services, portfolio, journal and contact routes.

## Demo-safety rules

- Never invent awards, press, ratings, review counts, years in business, staff biographies, certifications, addresses, partnerships, client names or real availability.
- Keep testimonial and portfolio stories clearly labelled as sample or conceptual content.
- Use the placeholder email and phone consistently; do not invent social handles.
- Do not present general travel, contract or budget guidance as legal, regulated or financial advice.
- Do not imply that stock-media subjects endorse LUMA.

## SEO rules

Give each page one primary intent, one useful H1, concise metadata and human-readable copy. Avoid keyword stuffing, thin city doorway pages, generic filler and repetitive wedding clichés.

Before publishing, search for stale brands, real private details, unsupported claims and inconsistent placeholders.
